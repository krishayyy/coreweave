    What was measured

    156 scenarios, three arms, three detection repeats per scenario. Every arm sees the same cases with the same detection rolls, so the arms are paired and the only difference between them is the method.

    The finding

    Every search system needs a signal for my premise may be wrong. Conventionally that signal is exhaustion, the fraction of the predicted area swept without contact. It cannot answer the question.

    Coming up empty happens just as often when you are looking in exactly the right place. This is the one result that compares two detectors on identical data rather than this system against a baseline we also wrote.

    Found within

    Searches that go well end in hours. A search still running on day three is looking in the wrong place, and day three is when a rescue becomes a recovery.

    Every arm, every family

    Wilson 95% intervals. Localised means the true location reached the top decile of belief at any point: it moves only when the agent reallocates belief correctly, and ignores detection luck.

    Paired differences

    Bootstrap over scenarios, repeats averaged within a scenario first. A cost of 2.8 points on correct-premise cases is real and significant, and those cases each carry a false lead designed to bait the system into moving. Break-even is a premise-error rate of 5.5 percent.
